This page collects short demonstrations of selected projects. The goal is to advance general physical intelligence and build systems that can reliably plan and execute in the real world. The first project is a robust locomotion controller that can be adapted across different robots and behaviors. The second goes a step further, learning the more abstract planning capability needed to search for the ball, approach it, kick, and continue playing, all within a single unified onboard policy.

Project 01

Fast agile locomotion on the Unitree G1

Simulation-trained locomotion controller deployed on multiple humanoid robots for fast movement, acceleration, terrain variation, and robustness to carried payloads.

Regular sprint on hardware.

Robots
Unitree G1 and Booster T1
Contribution
Simulation training, hardware transfer, and evaluation across speed, terrain, and payload changes.

Project 02

Autonomous ball kicking on the Booster T1

End-to-end reinforcement learning controller for autonomous goal scoring in a RoboCup-style setting, trained in simulation and deployed with onboard perception.

Continuous autonomous play: approach, align, kick, reset, repeat.

Controlled initial positions: close and far starts from left, middle, and right.

Robot
Booster T1
Sensing
Head-mounted RealSense camera
Compute
Onboard processing only
Result
26/30 goals from controlled initial positions
